STOP:bit: turn all three lights off

Block: stopbit_eteindre

What is it for?

Turns off the red, the amber and the green of the STOP:bit.

How to use it?

Put it at the end of a cycle, or when the program must stop the traffic light.

Good to know

  • The light stays off until the next STOP:bit block that turns a light on.

Example

The light goes red for 3 seconds, red and amber for 1 second, green for 3 seconds, then amber for 1 second, and starts again.
