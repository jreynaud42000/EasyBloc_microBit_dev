ACCESS:bit: wire servo motor on … buzzer on …

Block: accessbit_cablage

What is it for?

Tells which pins the barrier's servo motor and the buzzer of the ACCESS:bit are connected to when it is plugged in with wires.

How to use it?

Put it only once, in the ‘on start’ block, before the other ACCESS:bit blocks.

Choose the pin of the servo motor and the pin of the buzzer.

Good to know

  • Without this block, the board keeps Kitronik's pins: servo motor on P0, buzzer on P1.
  • On the Motor:bit, the servo motor prefers a G-VCC-S pin (P13 to P16, VCC selector on 5 V).

Example

Three Kitronik boards connected with wires to the Motor:bit. Button A: the light turns green, the barrier rises for 3 seconds, then red light and barrier down. The lamp turns on when it is dark.
