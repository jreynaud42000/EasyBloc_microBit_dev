ACCESS:bit: … the barrier

Block: accessbit_barriere

What is it for?

Raises or lowers the barrier of the Kitronik ACCESS:bit, moved by a servo motor.

How to use it?

First add the ACCESS:bit with File > Extensions: its blocks appear in the Kitronik category.

Choose raise or lower.

Good to know

  • The block waits 1 second, the time of the movement, then the program goes on.
  • Raise = 0°, lower = 90°. The servo motor is on pin P0.

Example

Press A: two short beeps, the barrier rises, stays up for 3 seconds, then a long beep and it lowers.
