STOP:bit: light … …

Block: stopbit_led

What is it for?

Turns a single light of the STOP:bit (red, amber or green) on or off, without touching the other two.

How to use it?

Choose the colour of the light, then on or off.

Good to know

  • For a real traffic light, the ‘STOP:bit: set traffic light to …’ block is simpler: it sets the three lights at once.
  • Red on P0, amber on P1, green on P2 (board screwed onto the micro:bit).

Example

The amber light blinks every half second, like a broken traffic light.
