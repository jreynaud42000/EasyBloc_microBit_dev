ACCESS:bit: … … times

Block: accessbit_bip

What is it for?

Sounds the buzzer of the ACCESS:bit, from 1 to 10 times.

How to use it?

Choose short beep (0.2 s) or long beep (0.8 s), then write the number of beeps.

Good to know

  • A silence of the same length follows each beep. The program waits for the end of the beeps before going on.
  • The buzzer is on pin P1.

Example

Press A: two short beeps, the barrier rises, stays up for 3 seconds, then a long beep and it lowers.
