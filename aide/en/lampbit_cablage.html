LAMP:bit: wire lamp on … light sensor on …

Block: lampbit_cablage

What is it for?

Tells which pins the lamp and the light sensor of the LAMP:bit are connected to when it is plugged in with wires.

How to use it?

Put it only once, in the ‘on start’ block, before the other LAMP:bit blocks.

Choose the pin of the lamp and the pin of the sensor.

Good to know

  • Without this block, the board keeps Kitronik's pins: lamp on P0, sensor on P1.
  • The sensor needs an analogue input: P0, P1, P2, P3, P4 or P10.
  • A pin of the LED matrix (P3, P4, P6, P7, P9, P10) turns the matrix off.

Example

Three Kitronik boards connected with wires to the Motor:bit. Button A: the light turns green, the barrier rises for 3 seconds, then red light and barrier down. The lamp turns on when it is dark.
